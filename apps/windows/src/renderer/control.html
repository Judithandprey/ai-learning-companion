Learning Companion

No AI is connected: captured frames and ink stay on this device, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Display to share

Choose the display you learn on, then press Start. Only that display is captured, and only until you press Stop.

    AI observation of this display

    These are this session's own bounds, not ChatGPT's quota: the AI stops by itself when either is reached, and the app never starts it again by itself. The last fifth of the requests is kept for your own circles and questions. One session lasts an hour and 100 requests at most in this version. The AI is given pictures of the display and what you type: speaking to it, and the sound of the lesson, are not connected in this version.

    Capture

    Not capturing.

    ChatGPT subscription

    Your own ChatGPT subscription, through the official Codex app server, which keeps the sign-in: this app never reads your password, tokens or cookies. ChatGPT is given pictures of a display only while the AI's session runs: your own Start, within the bounds set beside it.

    Ink that could not be saved

    This ink is kept in the app, not yet on this device's disk. Retry saving it, export it to a file you choose, or discard it. Closing the app keeps waiting for your choice.

    The app stays open until this ink is saved, exported or discarded.

      Saved ink

      Ink is saved on this device at every change, with pictures of what it was written over. Open a saved session to show it over the display being captured and keep editing it; Pictures shows what each stroke was written over.
