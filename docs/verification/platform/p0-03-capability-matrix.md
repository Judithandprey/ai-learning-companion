| Real provider (OneNote/Notability) | 0 | No account connected. |

`doc_basis` records how each claim is known (`documented`, `documented_absent`, `inferred`,
`undocumented`, `third_party_only`). The checker rejects a row whose status overstates its basis. A
`documented` or `documented_absent` row must cite at least one research claim whose verifier-corrected
status is `documented`, and at least one primary source. Forum sources are split three ways:
`F` = reply by an Apple DTS engineer (an Apple statement, not reference documentation); `E` = reply by
other Apple staff; `D` = developer posts with no Apple answer (never documentation).

## Decisions: preferred and fallback paths

**G1: input on the original page (Safari)**
- Preferred: a Safari web extension shipped inside our app (G1-01).
  - The content script provides the NAV/ASK/WRITE icons and separates `pen` from `touch` input (G1-10).
  - The background captures the frame with `tabs.captureVisibleTab` (G1-07).
  - A content script in the player frame reads `currentTime` for `media_position`. For Kaltura's
    cross-origin iframe this depends on G1-08, which is not tested.
  - `selection.submit` goes through `sendNativeMessage` to the native app extension's
    `SafariWebExtensionHandler`. That handler runs in a separate process from the containing app and
    hands the request over through an App Group container or the backend (G1-03).
  - The web role owns the content script. iOS owns the containing app and the handler.
- The containing app can detect whether the extension is enabled and deep-link to its settings on iPadOS 26.2+ (G1-05).
- Known in-page gaps: native-player fullscreen (G1-09), Canvas Student web views and Home Screen web
  apps (G1-11), and possibly simultaneous Pencil and finger input in WebKit (G1-10).
- Fallback 1: the G2 assistant window.
- Fallback 2: an in-app browser using `WKWebExtension` with a native Pencil layer (G1-12). It needs
  Xcode. Its risk is CalNet/Duo WebAuthn inside WKWebView.
- No-Mac route: a web-only extension can reach the iPad through the App Store Connect packager and
  TestFlight (G1-02). This requires the paid program. It is expected to have no native bridge. That is
  inferred from WWDC26 216 and the packager's web-only input; Apple does not state it (D1-03).

**Native Pencil and ink (own windows; serves G1 fallback, G2 and P0-11)**
- Preferred: in our own views, route Pencil to ink with a pencil-only recognizer while fingers keep
  operating the page (PEN-02). The pieces are documented; their interplay with WKWebView is not.
  Fallback: an explicit NAV mode that disables the ink layer (PEN-03), or annotation on a frozen frame.
- No public API to detect a paired Pencil was found (PEN-04). This is inferred from an API review, not
  an Apple statement. Start in finger-safe mode and switch after the first Pencil touch or hover.
  On-screen icons remain the required control. Squeeze and double tap are optional (PEN-05).
- The editable original is PKDrawing data, saved atomically at each stroke end (INK-01, INK-05). The
  AI layer is stored separately (INK-04). iPadOS 27 adds stable stroke IDs and recognition (INK-03).

**G2: native cross-app (Canvas Student, Notability)**
- Apple DTS says a floating overlay over other apps is not possible (G2-01). No public API for
  touch/Pencil interception in other apps exists; that part is inferred.
- PiP delivers no custom touch or Pencil input to the app; only system controls respond (G2-02).
- No global overlay may be promised.
- Preferred: a user-arranged side-by-side or Slide Over assistant (G2-03) plus G3 capture. The user
  circles and writes on captured frames inside our window.
- Secondary entry points: screenshot and share (G2-05, always available), drag and drop (G2-04),
  Visual Intelligence provider on iPadOS 27 with Apple Intelligence (G2-06), and squeeze → Shortcut
  (G2-07, experimental).

**G3: screen, audio, multiple devices**
- Preferred on iPadOS 27: in-process ScreenCaptureKit full-display capture, started through the system
  picker (G3-01).
  - Background modes: `screen-capture` and `audio`.
  - Audio session: `playAndRecord` + `mixWithOthers`, without `.defaultToSpeaker` (G3-02, G3-08).
  - The microphone is a separate stream chosen by the user in the picker (G3-04).
  - Each device timestamps on its own stream clock. Cross-device alignment needs a server offset,
    because no cross-device clock is documented (G3-10).
- Check first: `SCContentSharingPicker.isAvailable` (G3-12).
- Unproven and decisive: background survival (G3-02), and whether other apps' course audio reaches
  `.audio` (G3-03).
- Known or likely limits:
  - FairPlay: for system recording, Apple documents that FairPlay video is blacked out while its audio
    is included (G3-06). Whether our ScreenCaptureKit capture behaves the same, and whether that audio
    reaches `.audio`, is inferred. DT-G3-04 decides.
  - Course audio picked up by the mic: Apple documents no way to remove it (G3-09). DT-G3-05 measures
    the leakage.
  - Changing capture settings requires a new stream and leaves a gap (G3-11).
- Fallback: a ReplayKit broadcast extension on iPadOS 26.x, or on 27 if G3-02 fails (G3-07). On 27.0
  Apple marks it "No longer supported"; runtime on 27 is reported only by third parties, and DT-G3-07
  decides. After that: authorized captions, audio-only sessions and screenshot-driven sessions.

**Lifecycle (A14)**
- Switching to Safari or Canvas backgrounds the scene. That is not an end: the state is
  `active_background` (LC-01).
- Only our explicit End calls `stopCapture` and reaches `ended`. A user stop becomes
  `capture_stopped_user` and a system stop `capture_interrupted_system`, with the session still open
  (LC-02).
- A force-quit has no documented reliable callback (LC-03). The server derives
  `interrupted_unobserved` from heartbeat timeout, and the client reconciles from its local journal.
- `UISceneClosureConfirmation` asks before a window close (LC-04).
- A Live Activity is only a Lock Screen indicator (LC-05).
- After End, work continues through `BGContinuedProcessingTask` and background uploads (LC-06).
- Suspension and interruptions deactivate the audio session (LC-07). Restarting the mic from the
  background is expected to fail (inferred), so `mic_paused` asks the user to return (LC-08).

**G5: external notes**
- The editable original stays in the app (INK-01).
- Notability: hand off a PDF through the share sheet or Files. The state is only "prepared/shared,
  needs import" (G5-02, G5-03). No documented silent-write route was found (G5-01); DT-G5-04 checks
  Shortcuts.
- OneNote: Graph page creation counts as saved only after a 201 response plus a GET read-back. A lost
  response is reconciled by a title token (G5-04). It needs an Entra app registration and MSAL
  (G5-05). Ink goes as images, not InkML (G5-06).
- Keep is not a default target (G5-07).

## Contract observations for the lead (v0.1.0 unchanged; requests only)

1. **Page origin on the iOS bridge.**
   - Apple documents two keys in the native handler's `userInfo`: the message
     (`SFExtensionMessageKey`) and, on iPadOS 17+, a Safari profile UUID (`SFExtensionProfileKey`).
     No sender tab, URL or origin key is documented; that absence is inferred from documentation
     silence (G1-03, RV-01).
   - The contract README says native code obtains the origin from its trusted extension context. On
     iPad that context is the extension background (`runtime.onMessage` sender).
   - `BridgeRequest` is `additionalProperties: false`, so the origin cannot travel inside it.
   - Request: define a bridge envelope. The background fills it from `MessageSender`, never from
     content-script data.
   - Decide whether the allowlist is checked against the top-level page (`MessageSender.tab.url`) or
     the sending frame (`MessageSender.origin`, plus `frameId`). For content scripts inside a Kaltura
     player iframe the two differ (RV-02, inferred).
   - `AuthorizationContext` has one `authorized_origin`, but bCourses plus player iframes need more
     than one origin. This needs a list, or one context per origin.
   - Optionally bind the session to the profile UUID.
   - Alternative: validate origin on the backend only.
2. **Captured-frame honesty.**
   - Apple documents FairPlay video blackout for system recording, mirroring and AirPlay (D4-10).
   - Unverified for our paths: whether ScreenCaptureKit blacks out protected video (inferred) and
     whether `captureVisibleTab` does (undocumented, D1-14). A 2024 developer report describes cropped
     `captureVisibleTab` images on iPad.
   - `Frame` has no gap or quality flag; `Observation.gap_flags` already has `missing_frame` and
     `stale_frame`.
   - Request: a way to mark a frame as `capture_unverified` or `protected_region_black`, or a rule that
     `representation=screen_capture` requires a non-black check.
3. **Capture and session states.** The native side reports these as distinct facts (LC-01/02/07/08):
   `active_foreground`, `active_background`, `capture_stopped_user`, `capture_interrupted_system`,
   `mic_paused`, `offline` and `ended`.
   - The server derives `interrupted_unobserved` from heartbeat timeout, because a force-quit or
     jetsammed app cannot report it (LC-03).
   - 0.1.0 has no DeviceStream or session-state schema. This belongs to the planned HTTP/session
     contracts.
4. **Evidence basis.** `CapabilityResult` has no field for documentation basis or source list. This
   matrix wraps each result with `doc_basis` and `sources`. Adopt the wrapper, or add optional fields
   in a future version.
